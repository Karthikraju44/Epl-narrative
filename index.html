Narrative Fingerprints of the Premier League

We crawl football journalism, cluster it by the kind of story being told, and build a fingerprint of how the media talks about each club.

See resultsHow it works

Our angle

Topic clustering tells you what an article is about. We ask how the story is framed.

Typical approach

Group articles by topic: transfers, injuries, match results. Useful, but every team ends up with similar clusters.

Our approach

Group articles by narrative frame (crisis, title race, redemption, injury blow, hype), then compare clubs by the mix of frames and the tone of coverage.

Pipeline

Five stages, one Python script.

01

Crawl

Collect BBC Sport EPL article links, then download and parse title, date and body with polite delays.

02

Clean and tag

Normalise text and assign each article a primary club by weighted mention counts.

03

Vectorise

TF-IDF with unigrams and bigrams. Club names are removed so clusters capture narrative, not identity.

04

Frame and cluster

Seven narrative frames scored with editable keyword lists, relative to the average article. KMeans runs as an exploratory check.

05

Score and visualise

Sentence-level VADER sentiment per article, then a club × narrative heatmap and sentiment chart.

Results

98 BBC Sport articles about 15 Premier League clubs, crawled on 27 October 2026. Each article was labelled with one narrative frame.

Frames track tone

Average sentence-level sentiment is lowest for Injury & setbacks (0.12) and Crisis & pressure (0.13), and highest for Redemption (0.31), Individual spotlight (0.31) and Title race (0.30). The frames separate by tone without being told to.

Man Utd: a crisis profile

38% of its 26 articles fall in Crisis & pressure, its largest frame, around the manager’s position. No other club with 4+ articles has more than 17% except Crystal Palace.

Man City: success and appeals

Of 13 articles, Title race is largest (31%), followed by Discipline (23%, driven by the appeal story) and Individual spotlight (23%). No crisis coverage.

Liverpool and Spurs: revival

Both have 40% of coverage in Redemption & revival (5 articles each). Spurs also has 40% in Discipline. Crystal Palace leans to Crisis (2 of 4) and has the lowest average sentiment (0.13).

Limitations

This is a snapshot of one news cycle, so Man Utd stories (26 of 98) shape the picture. Clubs with fewer than 4 articles are left out of the charts, and clubs with 4 to 5 articles are indicative only. Frames come from editable keyword lists, and 24% of articles are Mixed/Unclear. TF-IDF + KMeans found only weak structure in earlier runs (silhouette about 0.05), grouping articles by format (live pages, match reports, awards) rather than by story, which is why frames use keyword lists instead.
